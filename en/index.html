Our services

Inside and out — all from one firm

Most clients start with the windows and then ask whether we can also do the solar panels, the gutters or the patio. We can: one point of contact, one invoice, one appointment in the diary.

Window cleaning

Our core trade. Window cleaners for households and businesses, inside and out, by squeegee or with purified water for a streak-free finish and no limescale marks.

  • Windows inside and out, including frames and sills
  • Working at height with a water-fed pole — up to the third floor without scaffolding
  • Conservatories, greenhouses, roof lights and glazed canopies
  • Roller shutters, awnings and insect screens
  • Shopfronts and display windows, including early morning before opening
  • Scheduled maintenance: monthly, six-weekly or quarterly

Indicative domestic price: €55 – €115 per visit · all prices

Solar panel cleaning

Dirty panels cost you yield. We clean with pure osmosis water and soft brushes — no harsh chemicals, no scratches and no pressure washing on your panels.

  • Cleaning with demineralised water, drying without streaks
  • Moss and grime along the lower edge and between panels
  • From ground level with a telescopic pole where possible — safer for your roof
  • Larger arrays on warehouses and farm buildings too

Indicative price: €5 – €10 per panel

Gutter cleaning

A blocked gutter causes damp patches on the facade and rotten fascia boards. Clearing it once a year — autumn is the right moment — avoids expensive repairs.

  • Leaves, moss and silt removed and taken away
  • Downpipes flushed and checked for blockages
  • Brackets and joints inspected, with a short report back to you
  • Worked from the ground where possible, no ladder against your facade

Indicative price: €3 – €5 per running metre

Facade cleaning & softwash

Green growth, algae and grime removed without damaging your facade. We prefer softwash (low pressure) over pressure washing, which protects pointing, render and insulating plaster.

  • Brick, render, decorative plaster, natural stone and cladding
  • Softwash kills the spores, so the growth stays away far longer
  • Optional sealing against moisture and regrowth
  • Separate elements too: carports, garden walls, concrete plinths

Indicative price: €8 – €22 per m², depending on the substrate

Roof cleaning & moss removal

Moss on roof tiles holds moisture and blocks your gutters. We remove the moss, treat against regrowth and clear up properly afterwards.

  • Brushed off or removed at low pressure, depending on your roof covering
  • Follow-up treatment with moss killer to prevent return
  • Gutters and downpipes cleared afterwards — loose moss would block them otherwise
  • Optional coating for tiled roofs

Priced individually after a site visit or from photos

Patio & driveway cleaning

Pressure washing of block paving, setts, concrete slabs, natural stone and concrete. Green growth off, jointing sand back in.

  • Pressure washing with a flat surface cleaner — no stripes, no blown joints
  • Weeds and moss between the joints dealt with at the same time
  • Re-sanding with kiln-dried or polymeric sand
  • Optional sealing against stains and faster regrowth

Indicative price: €3 – €7 per m² · fixed fee from €170

A common question, answered up front

No, we do not accept service vouchers. And for this work, that changes nothing.

Belgian service vouchers (dienstencheques / titres-services) are intended for domestic help indoors: cleaning, laundry, ironing and cooking. Window cleaning can be paid for with them, but only where it forms part of ordinary household maintenance — a separate visit covering just your windows falls outside the scheme.

And everything else we do — solar panels, gutters, facade cleaning, roof moss removal, pressure washing of patios and driveways, carpet and upholstery cleaning, post-construction cleaning and all business work — never qualifies for service vouchers in any case. This is specialist work with its own equipment and its own insurance.

What you do get from us: a proper invoice, work that is insured, and for sole traders and companies an expense that is fully deductible. If you are looking for help with the weekly housework using vouchers, an accredited service-voucher company will serve you better — and we would rather tell you that straight away.

Frequently asked questions

What clients ask us most

What does it cost to have your windows cleaned?

For a terraced house, expect €55 to €70 for the exterior; for a detached house, €85 to €115. Inside and out together runs between €85 and €150. The price depends on the number of windows, how accessible they are, and whether there is a conservatory or roof lights. You always get a fixed price before we begin.

How often should solar panels be cleaned?

Once every two years is enough for most roofs in the Kempen. Annual cleaning makes sense if you live near a busy road, a farm or a lot of trees, or if your panels sit at a shallow pitch — rain then washes them less effectively. If you can see a green or black band along the bottom edge of the panels, it is time.

When is the best time to have gutters cleared?

Late October to early December, once the leaves have fallen. If there are conifers or birches near the house, a second visit in spring is no luxury. Do not wait until water spills over the edge: by then the damage to facade and woodwork is usually already done.

Will you pressure wash my facade?

Preferably not. On render, decorative plaster and older pointing we use softwash: low pressure with a product that kills algae and moss spores. The result comes more slowly but stays clean far longer and does not damage your facade. We do use pressure washing on concrete, block paving and hard natural stone.

Do I need to be at home?

Not for outdoor work, as long as we can reach an outside tap and all sides of the house. For indoor work, yes, or you leave a key. Many regular clients give us a key or a code — we record that properly and use it only on the agreed days.

What happens if it rains?

Light rain is no problem for windows — we work with pure water, which dries without marks. In heavy rain, frost or strong wind we move outdoor work to the next suitable day. You hear about it that morning, not afterwards.

Do you accept service vouchers?

No. We invoice directly, with VAT. For businesses that is a fully deductible expense. Worth knowing: solar panels, gutters, facades, roofs, patios and post-construction cleaning never qualify for service vouchers anyway, not even with an accredited company.

How soon can you come?

In the Kempen usually within one to two weeks, a little longer in spring — March to June is our busiest period for windows, solar panels and patios. For urgent handovers or key exchanges we fit you in where we can.

Can I simply cancel my current cleaning contract?

A notice period of one to three months usually applies, depending on what your contract says. Send us your current agreement and we will look together at when a switch is possible, so there is no gap without maintenance.

Are you insured for working at height?

Yes. We carry public liability and completed-operations cover. For work at height we use telescopic poles from ground level wherever possible — safer for our people and for your gutters and facade. An insurance certificate is available on request.
